Features

nothing you don't.

QR Ticket Scanning

How it works

Up and running in 3 steps.

No long forms, no phone calls. AuditoriaX is designed to be instant.

🎓

Register your institution

Sign up in under 2 minutes. Your college gets a fully isolated admin space — auditoriums, events, members, and analytics are all yours.

📅

Create & publish events

Add auditoriums, set seat capacity, configure pricing, and publish events. Students see them in real-time the moment you hit publish.

🎟️

Book, pay & attend

Students pick their seat, pay if needed, and get a QR ticket by email. Gate staff scan and validate in seconds. That's it.
